## Title (30 chars max)

## Subtitle (30 chars max)

Nutrition, training, insight

## Description

Vora brings your nutrition, training, and body weight together into one calm, focused picture — so you can see whether what you do each day is actually moving you toward your goal.

TRACK YOUR NUTRITION
- Log meals in seconds with search powered by the OpenFoodFacts database
- Scan barcodes with your camera
- Save your own custom foods
- Calorie ring and macro bars show exactly where you stand against your daily targets
- Water tracking, one glass at a time

TRAIN WITH STRUCTURE
- Build your weekly split: Push Pull Legs, Upper/Lower, Full Body, or fully custom
- Log every set, rep, and weight with a built-in rest timer
- Personal records detected automatically
- Cardio sessions with duration and calories
- Optional Apple Health sync for workouts and weight

SEE YOUR PROGRESS
- Weight trend charts with selectable time ranges
- Weekly averages for calories and macros vs your targets
- Workout streaks that respect your rest days
- Goal projection: at your current rate, when you will reach your target weight
- One personalised insight every day, generated from your own data

BUILT TO RESPECT YOU
- Your data stays on your device and in your personal iCloud — no account, no ads, no selling your information
- Targets calculated from your own stats with the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, fully editable
- Designed for iPhone with full Dark Mode, Dynamic Type, and VoiceOver support

Whether you are cutting, maintaining, or building, Vora keeps the whole picture in one place — and tells you what it means.

- Barcode scanning and food search use the public OpenFoodFacts API.
