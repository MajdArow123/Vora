Vora Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 July 2026

Vora is a food, workout, and progress tracker for iPhone. It is built on a simple principle: your health data belongs to you.

What Vora stores, and where

Everything you log in Vora — meals, water, workouts, body weight, profile details, and targets — is stored locally on your device. Vora has no accounts, no server of its own, and no analytics or advertising SDKs. We cannot see your data, and we do not want to.

Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, Vora can save your workouts and body weight to Health and read weight, steps, and energy burned to keep your progress in sync. This is entirely optional, controlled by iOS permissions you can revoke at any time in Settings, and Health data is never sent anywhere by Vora.

Food search and barcode scanning

When you search for a food or scan a barcode, Vora sends only the search text or barcode number to the public OpenFoodFacts database to look up nutrition information. No personal information, identifiers, or health data are included in these requests.

Notifications

Weekly summary notifications are generated and scheduled entirely on your device.

Data deletion

Deleting the app deletes all data Vora stores. Data written to Apple Health remains under your control in the Health app.
